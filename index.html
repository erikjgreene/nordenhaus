The Norden Haus Experience

A quiet sanctuary crafted for gathering and reconnection.

Tucked into the peaceful birch and pine woods of Schuss Mountain, this newly renovated chalet pairs honest Nordic aesthetics with true Northern Michigan comfort.

Whether you’re warming up by the natural wood-burning fireplace after a day carving first tracks on the slopes, roasting marshmallows with the family under starry skies around the custom firepit, or setting out for the turquoise Caribbean-clear sandbars of Torch Lake, Norden Haus is your private 4-season basecamp.
